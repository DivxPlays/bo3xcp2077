# Black Ops Cyberpunk Zombies Mashup

This is a mashup combining Call of Duty: Black Ops III zombies mode with Cyberpunk 2077 elements. The core gameplay remains Black Ops III zombies mechanics, but enhanced with cyberpunk-themed weapons, enemies, environments and visual effects.

## Games Included
- **Primary Game**: Call of Duty: Black Ops III (zombies mode)
- **Companion Game**: Cyberpunk 2077 (for assets, themes and elements)

## Features
- Traditional Black Ops III zombies gameplay with cyberpunk enhancements
- Cyberpunk-themed weapons and equipment
- Night City-inspired environments and visual effects
- Enhanced enemy designs incorporating cyberpunk aesthetics
- New items and power-ups with cyberpunk flair

## How to Play
1. Install both Call of Duty: Black Ops III and Cyberpunk 2077
2. Launch the mashup through Melty
3. Enjoy classic zombies gameplay with a cyberpunk twist!
